Joining Bonus Terms: The joining bonus of ₹1,20,000 will be paid with your first month's salary. If you voluntarily resign within 12 months of joining, you shall refund the entire joining bonus amount. If you resign between 12-24 months, a pro-rated recovery will apply.

Exclusivity: During your employment with DataFlow Systems, you shall work on a full-time basis and shall not engage in any other employment or business activity without prior written approval.
