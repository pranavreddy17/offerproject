OFFER LETTER

Date: 15th January 2024

Dear Candidate,

We are pleased to offer you the position of Software Engineer at TechCorp India Pvt. Ltd.

Your annual CTC (Cost to Company) will be Rs. 8,00,000 (Eight Lakhs) per annum, structured as follows:

Fixed Pay: Rs. 5,60,000 per annum
Variable Pay: Rs. 1,40,000 per annum (performance bonus, subject to appraisal)
Joining Bonus: Rs. 1,00,000 (one-time)

The variable pay component is at the discretion of the company and is not guaranteed. It will be based on individual performance and company results.

Service Bond: You are required to serve a minimum service period of 2 years from the date of joining. In the event of early separation, you shall be liable to pay liquidated damages of Rs. 2,00,000 to the company.

Notice Period: Either party may terminate this employment by providing 90 days' notice or salary in lieu thereof. However, the company reserves the right to terminate your employment with 30 days' notice or immediately in case of misconduct.

Joining Bonus Clawback: The joining bonus of Rs. 1,00,000 shall be refunded in full if you leave the organization within 1 year of joining, and on a pro-rated basis if you leave within 2 years.

Training Cost Recovery: The company will invest approximately Rs. 3,00,000 in your training during the first 6 months. If you resign within 18 months of completing the training, you shall reimburse the company for the training cost on a pro-rated basis.

Non-Compete: During your employment and for a period of 12 months after separation, you shall not join a competitor company or engage in any competing business. The company defines competitors as any entity providing similar software services.

Moonlighting: You shall not engage in any other employment, business, or professional activity during the tenure of your employment without prior written approval from the management.

Please confirm your acceptance by signing and returning this letter within 7 days.

Regards,
HR Department
TechCorp India Pvt. Ltd.
